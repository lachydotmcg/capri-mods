# Holocall

Say "holo call" and she stays on a little call screen in the corner, glitchy face, call-line voice and all, until you say "hang up".

- Kind: code mod
- Made by: Issei

| Say | She says |
|---|---|
| holo call | Patching you through. |
| start a holo call | Patching you through. |
| hang up | Hanging up. |
| end the holo call | Hanging up. |
| what's a holo call | What it does and how to start it |

The call screen comes up at the top right of your main screen and stays there until you hang up. It shows her face in a square with her name, voice bars and the call timer. She blinks, glances around between lines and looks at you while she talks. Her mouth opens and closes with her voice under a band of interference, and her head bobs and tilts as she goes. What she says changes her face too: wide eyes when she's excited, a head tilt when she asks something, a squint when she laughs. Your clicks go straight through it, and it hides while a fullscreen game or video is open.

For the call, her voice gets her own robot and glitch effects turned up a little, with quiet line static and a chirp when the call connects and when it ends. Hanging up puts her voice back to how your settings have it. Your settings are never changed, so nothing sticks after Capri restarts.

"Hang up", "end this call" and "disconnect" only end a holocall while one is on, so they still work for her own call mode the rest of the time.

To follow her voice and change it, the mod reads a few of Capri's own private settings. If a Capri update renames them, the call screen still works, but her mouth won't follow her voice and her voice stays normal until the mod is updated. Windows only.
