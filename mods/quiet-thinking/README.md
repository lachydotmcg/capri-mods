# Quiet Thinking

Hides Capri's empty chat box while she thinks, so it only pops up once she has something to say.

- Kind: code
- Made by: Potato

While she's thinking, the empty box is hidden and three small pulsing dots show in its place. When her reply is ready the box appears with its usual opening glitch.

- Say "thinking dots off" to hide the dots too, and "thinking dots on" to bring them back.
- Her "Still thinking" note on a slow reply still shows.
- The box is cloaked, not moved or closed, so her own drawing is untouched. Turning the mod off brings it straight back.
