# Talia Yang Capri

A fan-made skin for [Capri Desktop](https://github.com/lachydotmcg/capri-desktop) that replaces Capri's appearance with Talia Yang from *Cyberpunk: Edgerunners II*.

The skin includes 43 sprite strips covering standing, walking, sitting, lying down, and other states. Talia wears her mask, fully zipped jacket, and silver boots with two red spikes each.

Important: I chose to keep Talia’s jacket fully zipped in every sprite because I want this mod to reflect my preference for a non-sexualized portrayal of the character.

I used AI to create and refine the sprites and preview image, and to assemble and check the skin package. I directed the design and tested the skin in Capri on Windows. Some animation states still need testing.
## Installation

1. Download [talia-capri.capri-skin](talia-capri.capri-skin).
2. Open **Capri Mainframe → CAPRI → Mods → Import mod**.
3. Select the downloaded file. enable **Talia Yang Capri** under **Installed**.
