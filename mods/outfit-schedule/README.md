# Outfit Schedule

Changes Capri's skin by time of day, day of the week or date. Neon Sign at night, the Santa hat in December, Sakura on weekends.

- Kind: code mod
- Made by: Issei
- Needs: Capri 0.8.6 or newer

| Say | She says |
|---|---|
| wear Neon Sign at night | Okay. Neon Sign at night. |
| wear the crimson Santa hat in December | Okay. Santa Hat Crimson on top in December. |
| wear Sakura on weekends | Okay. Sakura on weekends. |
| no skin during the day | Okay. My own look during the day. |
| wear the business suit on weekdays from 9am to 5pm | Okay. Business Suit from 9am to 5pm on weekdays. |
| what's my outfit schedule | Reads the rules, top one first |
| pause the outfit schedule | Outfit schedule paused. I'll keep wearing this. |
| clear the outfit schedule | That removes all three rules. Are you sure? |

When a rule starts she switches and says so once, like "Night shift. Switching to Neon Sign." When it ends she goes back to what she was wearing before. Accessories like hats and halos go on top of whatever she's wearing.

It only changes her look when a rule starts or ends. If you pick a skin yourself, she keeps it until the next one. When two rules want different skins, the one higher on the list wins. Specific rules go above general ones, and you can reorder them on the Options page. You can also add and delete rules there.

It understands times of day (at night, in the morning, from 9 to 5), days (on weekends, on weekdays, on Mondays and Fridays), months (in December) and a few holidays (on Christmas, on Halloween). It checks every minute, and when your PC wakes or unlocks. If a skin on the schedule has been uninstalled, she skips that rule and tells you once. Windows only.
