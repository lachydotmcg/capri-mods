# Medal Clip

Say "clip that" and she clips your screen with Medal, then tells you if it saved. If Medal isn't installed or open, she says so.

- Kind: code mod
- Made by: Palewoods

Works with any line that has "clip that" in it, like "Capri, clip that". She skips "don't clip that" and "how do I clip that". She reads your clip hotkey from Medal's own settings, so a changed hotkey still works (F8 by default). After pressing it, she watches Medal's clips folder (C:\Medal\Clips or Videos\Medal\Clips) for up to 25 seconds to confirm the clip saved. If you moved your capture folder, she tells you she can't check.

This mod takes "clip that" over from her built-in game clips, so Medal does the clipping instead. Code mods run C# on your PC, so she asks before turning one on. This one only presses your Medal clip hotkey and looks in your clips folder. Code mods are Windows only for now.

Tip: want "Capricorn, clip that" to work too? Add Capricorn as one of her names with a settings mod (see Custom Names).
