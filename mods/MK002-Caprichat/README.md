# MK002 Caprichat

A private chat window for Capri

- Kind: code
- Made by: Mirit0
- Version: 2.1.6

## Note: when installing or turning on the mod, Capri freezes for a few seconds

## What it does

Ask Capri to open her chat ("open Caprichat", "let's DM", "show our chat") and the window appears.

**Two-way, fully synced**
- Everything you say by voice and everything Capri says shows up in the chat, and anything you type in the chat goes to Capri as a normal turn — including her spoken reply.
- Live sync from her transcripts and conversation history, with duplicate detection so nothing is listed twice.
- Chats are split by date
- Older days open read-only; reply to a message to write into them.

**Messaging**
- Reply to any message and Capri answers with the quoted thread as context.
- Attach images to a message, by button or by paste.
- Search the whole archive.
- Emoji reactions on any message, with recent emojis, an emoji picker, installable emoji packs (separate mods).

**Capri reacts back**
- She reacts to your messages and her own with her own chosen emoji
- React to one of her messages and she responds to it.
- She can favorite messages she likes (based on personality and memories)

**Window and settings**
- Settings panel: chat font size, custom font file, and show/hide for screen observations and job notices (both hidden by default).
- All state — messages, reactions, stars, recents, preferences — is stored locally in the mod's own folder.
