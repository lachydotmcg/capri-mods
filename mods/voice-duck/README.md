# Voice Duck

Turns Capri's voice down while your game, music or video is loud, so she doesn't shout over it, and brings it back up when things go quiet.

It listens to your speakers only while she isn't talking, so her own voice never ducks her, and there's a gap between "loud" and "quiet" so she doesn't flicker at the edge. Change her volume in her settings, or mute her with the Mute Button mod, and that simply becomes her new normal.

**Options:** how loud counts as loud · how far she ducks (% of her normal volume)

- Kind: code mod
- Made by: Quaisar_THM
- Needs: Capri with mod API 3 (0.8.7 or newer), Windows
- Permissions: music, voice

No network access, no keys, nothing sent anywhere.
