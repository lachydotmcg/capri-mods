# Soundboard

Shortcuts that play sounds, and Capri reacts to each one. It comes with six sounds of its own, and you can add your own.

- Kind: code mod
- Made by: Issei
- Needs: Capri 0.8.6 or newer

| Say | She says |
|---|---|
| play the drum sting | Ba-dum tss. (and plays it) |
| play the air horn | Plays it, and she dances |
| list sounds | I've got 6 sounds: air horn on Ctrl+Alt+1, buzzer on Ctrl+Alt+2, ... |
| open the soundboard | Here's the soundboard. |
| soundboard off | Soundboard off. The shortcuts are free again. |
| soundboard on | Soundboard on. 6 shortcuts ready. |

The sounds it comes with are an air horn, a buzzer, a cheer, a drum sting, a glitch zap and a record scratch. The mod makes them itself the first time it runs, so there's nothing to download or license. Ctrl+Alt+1 to Ctrl+Alt+9 play the first nine sounds in name order.

To add your own, open its Options page and click Open sounds folder, then drop WAV files in (Capri plays up to 30 seconds of each). The Options page has a row for each sound: its shortcut, volume, a Play button and what she does when it plays (nothing, glitch, grin, dance or say a line). A shortcut can be Ctrl, Alt or Win with a key, or an F key. If another app already has a shortcut, the row says so. Delete a sound you don't want and it stays deleted. Windows only.
