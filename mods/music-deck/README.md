# Music Deck

Swaps her mini player for a glass music deck that takes its colours from the song's cover, with a live visualizer, synced lyrics, history, app switching, a spinning mini disc, a sleep timer, volume and hotkeys.

- Kind: code mod
- Made by: Vocyn
- Version: 1.1.0
- Permissions: music, hotkeys, chat, tools
- Needs: Capri 0.8.7 or newer. Windows only (on Mac and Linux nothing reads music yet).

## What it does

- **Cover colours**: the deck's glow, seek bar and play disc take the two strongest colours from the cover, and the cover's colours wash softly behind the glass. Turn it off for her pink and cyan.
- **Live visualizer**: bars or a wave along the bottom, from what your speakers are actually playing. The play disc's halo and the edge glow breathe with the music.
- **Synced lyrics**: fold the deck open (the arrow, or double-click it) for lyrics from lrclib.net that follow the song line by line. The line playing grows and fills in left to right as it's sung, lines already sung fade back, and long lines wrap instead of being cut off. Intros and pauses in the words show three dots that fill as they run out. Click a line to jump there, or scroll to look ahead; it eases back to the song a few seconds later. A ticker under the folded deck shows the line playing now.
- **Lyrics that fit**: slowed, sped-up and nightcore edits get the original song's lyrics, stretched to the edit's length. If a song's lyrics run early or late, the − and + under them move them a quarter second at a time, and it's remembered for that song. Lyrics without times scroll along with the song.
- **A seat for her**: she sits on the deck's top edge like a window, and hops onto it over the cover when it shows (turn that off in Options). Its title and app chip move out from under her legs.
- **History**: the last 40 songs, kept between runs. Click one to search for it in Spotify, or in YouTube Music for other apps.
- **Sources**: every app playing something. Pick one to follow it instead of whatever Windows picks.
- **Mini disc**: the minimise button turns it into a spinning record with the song's progress round its edge and the music as rays. Click to play or pause, double-click to open the full deck.
- **Controls**: play/pause, skip, seek with a time preview, shuffle and repeat (for apps that take them), Spotify's heart, mute, and scroll anywhere on the deck for that app's volume.
- **Sleep timer**: right-click > Sleep timer to pause the music after 15 to 90 minutes or when the song ends. A moon shows the time left.
- **Capri knows**: she's told what's playing and the lyric playing now, so "what song is this?" and "sing along" work. She can also open the lyrics, switch to the mini disc or set a sleep timer when you ask.

Drag it anywhere; it snaps to screen edges and remembers where you left it. It shows while music plays, fades out 45 seconds after a pause (or stays up, in Options), and keeps out of full-screen games and videos.

## Hotkeys

| Keys | Does |
|---|---|
| Ctrl+Alt+Space | Play / pause |
| Ctrl+Alt+Right / Left | Next / previous |
| Ctrl+Alt+Up / Down | Volume up / down |
| Ctrl+Alt+L | Open the lyrics |
| Ctrl+Alt+M | Show / hide the deck |

## Privacy

Lyrics are looked up on lrclib.net with the song's title, artist, album and length, and kept in the mod's folder so each song is only looked up once. Turn lyrics off in Options to send nothing.
