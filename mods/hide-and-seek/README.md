# Hide and Seek

Capri hides somewhere on your desktop and you find her. Click or pat her when you spot her.

- Kind: code mod
- Made by: Issei
- Needs: Capri 0.8.6 or newer

| Say | She says |
|---|---|
| let's play hide and seek | Close your eyes. Count to five. |
| hide and seek hard | Hard mode. Close your eyes. Count to five. |
| give me a hint | I'm sitting on something you have open. |
| i give up | Ha! I was on the taskbar. You looked for 1 minute. |
| what's my best time | Your best time, your best on hard, and how often each of you has won |

She hides on one of your open windows, the taskbar, a bottom corner or your other screen, and never in the same spot twice in a row. On hard mode she shrinks to half her size, then goes back to normal when the game ends. After 30 seconds she makes a little sound, after a minute she offers a hint, and after three minutes she comes out and wins. Finding her tells you your time and whether it's a new best. Afterwards she walks back to where she was. Windows only. To pick a window, it looks at which programs have big windows open (their names only; nothing leaves your PC), and she may say that name when you give up.
