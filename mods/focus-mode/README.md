# Focus Mode

Pomodoro rounds with Capri. While you work she sits down and keeps quiet, and on your break she gets up and dances.

- Kind: code mod
- Made by: Issei
- Needs: Capri 0.8.6 or newer

| Say | She says |
|---|---|
| focus | Focus on. 25 minutes. I'll be quiet. |
| focus for 50 minutes with 10 minute breaks | Focus on. 50 minutes. I'll be quiet. |
| how long left | 15 minutes left in round 1. Then a 5 minute break. |
| pause focus | Paused with 25 minutes left. |
| resume focus | Back on. 25 minutes left. |
| skip the break | Break's over. Round 3, 25 minutes. Back to it. |
| stop focus | Focus off. 60 minutes of work, 2 full rounds. Today: 2 rounds, 1 hour. |
| focus stats | How much you focused today and this week, and your streak |

Ctrl+Alt+F starts or pauses it, and her right-click menu has Start or pause focus and Stop focus. Rounds are 25 minutes with 5 minute breaks and a 15 minute break every 4 rounds; change any of these on its Options page.

While a round is on she turns on quiet, and turns off check-ins and wandering. When focus ends, those go back to how they were. A setting you change yourself during focus is left as you set it. She warns you a minute before a break ends. If you step away during a round, the clock pauses and she tells you how long you were gone when you get back. A session survives a Capri restart. Other mods can tell when you're focusing. Windows only.
